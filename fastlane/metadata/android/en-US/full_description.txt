TalQ is a native client for Nextcloud Talk, built with Kotlin and Jetpack Compose. It is the Android sibling of TalQ for desktop. Topics (Talk's threads) are first-class: each topic is a tab inside its conversation, with its own place, draft, unread count and notification level.

<b>What it does</b>
<ul>
<li>Sign in through your browser (Nextcloud Login Flow v2). Only an app password is stored, encrypted with a key held in the Android Keystore, and it is revoked when you sign out (if your server can be reached then).</li>
<li>Conversations with unread counts, mention markers and presence dots on one-to-one chats.</li>
<li>Topics as tabs, a "next unread topic" button, new topics, and a notification level per topic.</li>
<li>Messages with formatting, mentions, replies, reactions and image previews. A message that could not be sent stays with Retry.</li>
<li>Files: send photos, videos and files (photos shrunk and location removed unless you send the original), uploads in the background, share into TalQ from other apps, in-app video and voice playback, and a Shared files view.</li>
<li>Notifications through UnifiedPush (for example ntfy) and your server's web push. A muted topic stays silent.</li>
<li>One-to-one audio and video calls with TalQ for desktop and the Nextcloud web app: incoming calls ring like phone calls, also over the lock screen; mute, camera, speaker and Bluetooth; the call goes on with the screen off. Needs Talk's high-performance backend on the server.</li>
<li>Optional shift status from your organisation's ERP.</li>
<li>Four themes, as on desktop (Ember, Warm, Vivid, Paper), or following the phone's light/dark setting.</li>
</ul>

Group calls are not there yet.

TalQ is an independent client and is not affiliated with Nextcloud GmbH. It needs a Nextcloud server with Talk.
